From the Padma River to the Gulf of Finland

I'm a final-year Civil Engineering student specialising in Hydraulic Engineering Construction at Admiral Makarov State University of Maritime and Inland Shipping, studying on a Russian Government Scholarship.

I grew up in Pabna, Bangladesh, on the Padma River, where riverbank erosion and sedimentation shape people's lives every year. That's what drew me to water. Today I design the structures that protect ports and study how sediment fills the channels ships depend on.

Before coming to Russia I earned a four-year Diploma in Civil Technology and worked as an engineer on a construction site in Dhaka. I learned Russian from zero and now study engineering in it.

I want to build coasts and rivers that protect people, not just ports.

Research and writing

My thesis and my first article both deal with how to keep a port calm and its channel deep.

Bachelor's thesis · 2027

Protective structures and the approach channel of a river-mouth port

Design of breakwaters and the approach channel for a port at a river mouth, taking the sediment that fills the channel into account. Case study: the Luga River mouth in the Gulf of Finland.

  • 1Wind, wave and river-sediment input data
  • 2Breakwater layout and armour sizing
  • 3Approach channel design and infilling estimate
  • 4Comparison of options and recommendations
Student article · 2026

Design of a port breakwater

«Проектирование оградительного мола морского порта» · supervisor P.V. Belyakov, PhD (Eng.), Associate Professor

The article compares natural rock and concrete tetrapods as armour for a rubble-mound breakwater, sizing each unit with the Hudson formula:
