I'm a final-year Civil Engineering student specialising in Hydraulic Engineering Construction at Admiral Makarov State University of Maritime and Inland Shipping in Saint Petersburg. I study on a Russian Government Scholarship.

I grew up in Pabna, Bangladesh, on the Padma River, where riverbank erosion and sedimentation shape people's lives. That's what drew me to hydraulic engineering. Today my work focuses on port protective structures, approach channels and how sediment fills them.

Before coming to Russia I earned a four-year Diploma in Civil Technology and worked as an engineer on a construction site in Dhaka. I'm now looking for internships and a master's programme in coastal, river and water resources engineering, in Europe, Asia or the Middle East.

Research and writing

Bachelor's thesis · 2027

Protective structures and the approach channel of a river-mouth port

Design of breakwaters and the approach channel for a port at a river mouth, taking sediment infilling of the channel into account. Case study: the Luga River mouth in the Gulf of Finland.

Methods: wave and sediment input data, breakwater armour sizing, channel infilling estimate, comparison of layout options.

Student article · 2026

Design of a port breakwater

«Проектирование оградительного мола морского порта»

Compares rock and concrete tetrapod armour for a rubble-mound breakwater, sizing the armour units with the Hudson formula and Russian and international design codes.

Supervisor: P.V. Belyakov, PhD (Eng.), Associate Professor, Department of Waterways and Hydrographic Surveys.
